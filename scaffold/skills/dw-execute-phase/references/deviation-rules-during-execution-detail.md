# Deviations during execution

Record material adjustments in deviations.md with task, reason, affected files and outcome.

- Complete functionality needed to satisfy the approved requirements and applicable project constraints; do not broaden product scope.
- Resolve discoverable ambiguity by inspecting code and previous decisions. Ask only when materially different product/architecture choices remain; block dependent work while continuing independent authorized work.
- A conflict with a locked architectural decision requires the project's ADR/decision process. Do not silently override it.
- Repair internal plan inconsistencies and split/reorder work within approved scope; larger estimates alone do not require approval.
